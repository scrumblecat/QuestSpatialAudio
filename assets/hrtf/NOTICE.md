The generated `src/hrtf_data.cpp` contains data derived from diffuse-field
equalized KEMAR head-related impulse responses measured by **Bill Gardner and
Keith Martin, MIT Media Laboratory (1994)**. The 48 kHz responses are resampled
with a windowed-sinc filter. The original archive is not bundled in this
repository; it is only needed to regenerate the checked-in table.

Citation: Bill Gardner and Keith Martin, *HRTF Measurements of a KEMAR
Dummy-Head Microphone*, MIT Media Lab Perceptual Computing Technical Report
280, 1994.

Source and usage terms: https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR.html
Archive: https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR/diffuse.zip
